Must Try Fall Recipes!

Pumpkin Spice Latte

This classic fall drink is a must-try! Made with espresso, steamed milk, pumpkin puree, and a blend of spices, it's the perfect way to warm up on a chilly autumn day.

Ingredients
  • espresso
  • steamed milk
  • pumpkin puree
  • cinnamon
  • nutmeg
  • cloves
  • sugar
 Directions:
  1. Brew a shot of espresso.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine pumpkin puree, sugar, and spices. Heat until warm.
  3. Add the espresso to the pumpkin mixture and stir.
  4. Pour steamed milk into the mixture and top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

Apple Cider Donuts

These soft and fluffy donuts are infused with apple cider and coated in cinnamon sugar. They're a delicious treat for any fall gathering! Best served with a cup of coffee and maybe some ice cream.

Ingredients
  • apple cider
  • flour
  • sugar
  • cinnamon
  • baking powder
  • eggs
  • milk
 Directions:
  1. In a saucepan, reduce apple cider until thickened.
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, cinnamon, and baking powder.
  3. Add eggs and milk to the dry ingredients and mix until combined.
  4. Fold in the reduced apple cider.
  5. Pour the batter into a donut pan and bake at 350°F for 12-15 minutes.
  6. Once cooled, coat the donuts in cinnamon sugar.

Butternut Squash Soup

This creamy soup is made with roasted butternut squash, onions, garlic, and a touch of cream. A savory soup best paired with a crusty bread. It's a comforting dish that's perfect for fall evenings.

Ingredients
  • butternut squash
  • onions
  • garlic
  • vegetable broth
  • cream
 Directions:
  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Cut the butternut squash in half, remove seeds, and roast until tender.
  2. In a pot, sauté onions and garlic until translucent.
  3. Add the roasted squash and vegetable broth to the pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Use an immersion blender to puree the soup until smooth.
  5. Stir in cream and season with salt and pepper to taste.

Caramel Apple Pie

This classic dessert features a flaky pie crust filled with spiced apples and drizzled with rich caramel sauce. It is a perfect way to end any fall meal! Best served warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients
  • apples
  • sugar
  • cinnamon
  • nutmeg
  • pie crust
  • caramel sauce
 Directions:
  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Prepare the pie crust in a pie dish.
  2. In a bowl, mix sliced apples with sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
  3. Pour the apple mixture into the pie crust and cover with a top crust.
  4. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
  5. Allow to cool slightly before drizzling with caramel sauce.
